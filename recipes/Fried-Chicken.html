Fried Chicken

Crispy white meat chicken tenders meant for sharing

These chicken strips are versatile, and easy to meal prep for busy nights. With just a ffew ingredients you can create a crunchy coating that will bring everyone back for more. Add these to a sandwhich, toss them in a salad, use them on pizza or eat them on their own. The possibilities are endless.

Ingredients

  • 2-2/3 c crushed saltines (about 80 crackers)
  • 1 tsp garlic salt
  • 1/2 tsp dried basil
  • 1/2 tsp paprika
  • 1/8 tsp pepper
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 c 2% milk
  • 1-1/2 pounds boneless skinless chicken breasts, cut into 1/2-inch strips
  • oil for frying

Directions

  1. In a shallow bowl combine the crackers, salt, basil, paprika, and pepper mix well
  2. In a separate bowl combine the egg and milk beat well
  3. Dip the chicken in the egg mixture, then into the cracker mixture
  4. Heat oil to 375F degrees. Fry the chicken a few at a time, for 2-3 mins on each side or until golden brown. Drain on paper towels
