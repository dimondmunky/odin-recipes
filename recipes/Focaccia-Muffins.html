Focaccia Muffins

A simple Focaccia recipe divided into muffins for added variety

These muffins are soft on the inside and crunchy on the outside. Using a muffin tin you can easily customize each muffin or make a batch of your favorite variety. This is a simple bread that requires little kneading, the recipe is easy to follow and fun.

Ingredients

    Focaccia Muffins
  • 1 1/4 tsp active dry yeast
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 1 1/4 c warm water
  • 2 1/2 c bread flour
  • 2 tsp sea salt
  • 1/4 c extra-virgin olive oil
    • Toppings (ex. Rosemary Garlic)
  • 1/4 c extra virgin olive oil
  • 2 tbsp melted butter
  • 2-3 sprigs of rosemary minced
  • 3 garlic cloves minced
  • 2 tbsp finishing salt

Directions

    Day One
  1. Mix the yeast, honey, and water together in a large bowl. Allow to sit for 5-10 mins until the mixture gets foamy and yeasty.
  2. Add the flour and salt to the bowl, and mix everything until a rough dough forms. The dough may still seem wet, but it should hold together.
  3. Drizzle about 2 tbsp of olive oil on top of the dough, and gently turn the dough in the oil so it's coated. Cover the bowl with plastic wrap and chill in the fridge overnight, at least 8 hours. The dough will double in size. Alternatively, cover the bowl and let sit at room temp for at least 4 hours, or until doubled in size. Continue with next step.
    Day two
  1. Drizzle an additional 2 tbsp of olive oil on the dough and give the dough a quick knead to deflate it in the bowl.
  2. Turn the dough out onto a lightly oiled counter. Pat it into a rough rectangle (the dough wil still be a little sticky).
  3. Using a sharp knife or bench scraper, divide the dough in half, and then cut six pieces from each half for a total of twwelve pieces of dough
  4. Grease a muffin pan with olive oil. Divide the dough into the muffin tins. The dough does not have to be perfectly round, but it should fit snugly into the tins.
  5. Allow the dough to rise, covered, for 2 hours.
  6. Pre-heat the oven to 450F degrees. Poke holes all over the top of the dough-tyhe holes should reach the bottom. Use oiled hands if the dough is sticky
  7. Bake for 20 minutes, or until golden brown
  8. Prepare the topping by heatinng the oil and butter in a pan over medium heat. Once the oil is shimmering turn off the heat and add the rosemary and garlic. Swirl it around to quickly cook the rosemary and garlic, if it starts to burn transfer the mixture to a bowl.
  9. Brush the top of the muffins with the topping mixture and sprinkle with finishing salt
